# Launch Security Review — client-facing offer draft

**For developers and founders building applications with AI tools**

## Before your app goes live, check what could go wrong

An app can look finished and still expose private information, allow the wrong user to access an account, or contain vulnerable dependencies. We review the parts you authorize, explain what we find, and help you decide what to fix first.

### What you receive

- **Scope review:** We confirm what you own and what we are permitted to test.
- **Risk assessment:** We examine relevant application behavior and, when provided with authorized read-only repository access, can separately review code, dependencies, and configuration.
- **Proof-backed findings:** We show what was actually confirmed, what is only suspected, and what was not tested.
- **Plain-English repair plan:** Each validated issue includes why it matters and a practical next step.
- **Optional retest:** After your team makes a fix, we can check whether that issue is still reproducible during a newly authorized test window.

### Suitable for

- AI-assisted or vibe-coded web applications
- MVPs preparing for their first customers
- SaaS founders who want an independent security review before release
- Small teams without a dedicated application security function

### The assessment process

1. **Tell us what you built.** Share the app's purpose, technology stack and an authorized staging environment.
2. **Approve the testing scope.** We verify ownership and agree on exact assets, time limits, test methods and a stop contact.
3. **We review the authorized application.** Security testing is supervised and designed to minimize disruption.
4. **You receive a prioritized report.** Verified issues, suggested changes, limitations and exclusions are clearly identified.
5. **Fix and retest if needed.** We record whether a reported issue is fixed, still present or cannot be confirmed.

### What we do not promise

A security assessment does not guarantee that an application has no vulnerabilities. We do not test systems you cannot authorize, and we do not attempt disruptive or invasive techniques without separate explicit permission.

### What we need before scheduling

- Proof you own or are authorized to commission testing of the exact application and assets
- An agreed Rules of Engagement document and testing time window
- A safe staging environment and test accounts where feasible
- Any known restrictions, sensitive integrations, or critical business periods

### Offer availability

**Controlled pilot only.** We are preparing the underlying testing workflow and its safety checks. No public automated scan or production-ready customer portal is being offered at this stage. Launch dates and service-level commitments are not yet established.

This draft is marketing copy for future approval, **not** a published service claim. A qualified human operator must verify each assessment and approve every client report.
